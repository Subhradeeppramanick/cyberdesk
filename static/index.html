Complaint auto-triage

Pick one of the bundled fictional sample complaints. CyberDesk routes it to a category with transparent, rules-based reasoning and extracts indicators (URLs, UPI handles, etc.).

This demo does not accept typed or pasted complaints. Please do not enter real complaints or personal data.

Suspicious URL analyser

Scores the text of a link with structural phishing heuristics (brand impersonation, risky TLD, punycode, @ trick...). In this demo it runs offline: nothing is fetched, resolved or stored, and it never looks up people. Do not enter links from real complaints.

Bank branch lookup (IFSC)

Validates an IFSC format and resolves the bank branch from a small bundled sample (try SBIN0000001, HDFC0000123 or ICIC0000001). IFSC codes identify branches, not people.
