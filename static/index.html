Complaint auto-triage

Paste a complaint narrative. CyberDesk routes it to a category with transparent, rules-based reasoning and extracts indicators (URLs, IPs, UPI handles, phone numbers, IFSC codes).

Suspicious URL analyser

Checks the infrastructure of a link found in a complaint: structural phishing heuristics, live DNS resolution and TLS certificate expiry. It never looks up people.

Bank branch lookup (IFSC)

Validates an IFSC from a mule-account lead and resolves the bank branch against public RBI-published data. IFSC codes identify branches, not people.

CyberDesk is an original hackathon demonstration project. It is not an official police system, it does not store or process real complaints, and it cannot be used to report an actual crime.

To report a real cybercrime in India, use the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) or call the national helpline 1930.
